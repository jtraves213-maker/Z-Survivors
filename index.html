Up to four fighters share one XP bar. Gamepads take the last seats (two pads in a 4-player game drive P3 and P4). Pick Goku and Vegeta together to unlock fusion. Once Goku reaches Super Saiyan 3 and Vegeta reaches Majin Vegeta, both players press their fuse key within 5 seconds of each other. It's a coin flip whether you get Gogeta or Vegito. A fusion lasts 30 seconds and recharges in 2 minutes. As Gogeta, Goku's player aims and fires while Vegeta's player steers; Vegito flips it.
